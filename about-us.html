About Us

About Us

Green Cycle Platform connects waste generators with recycling companies and scrap management companies, while supporting organizations with environmental, consulting, certification, and training services.

About Green Cycle Platform

  • Establishing and operating a direct interactive platform that connects entities producing different types of waste directly with recycling companies and scrap management companies, with the goal of reducing and eliminating public landfills.
  • Providing services that improve environmental and sustainable practices within organizations, helping buildings meet environmental and social responsibility policy requirements in a technical, effective, and measurable way that supports a competitive carbon footprint.
  • Granting Green Compliance Accreditation periodically to raise the standing of entities locally and globally.
  • Providing consulting services in environmental protection and energy efficiency.
  • Providing accredited training programs in Kuwait and internationally across several fields, including environmental programs, energy conservation, artificial intelligence, and others.

To learn more about Sustainable Solutions, visit: smarttechkw.com

Initiative Objectives

  • Contributing to supporting leading companies in achieving their corporate social responsibility (CSR) goals. Our platform strengthens this responsibility through statistics and performance reports related to sustainability in waste management, improving building energy efficiency, conserving water, and improving indoor air quality.
  • The platform creates modern financing opportunities by offering diverse financial incentives to encourage the public to dispose of waste properly, improve energy efficiency, conserve water, and achieve indoor air quality within facilities. Participants can receive various incentives when they improve their environmental practices, whether personally or through their companies, through Green Cycle Platform.
  • Contributing to raising awareness of the importance of reducing waste in Kuwait landfills by issuing an international certificate to preserve Kuwait's landfills for every individual and every institution participating in Green Cycle Platform.
  • Contributing to reducing Kuwait's annual landfill waste, which exceeds 20 million tonnes, eliminating 20% of it by 2028 and eliminating it completely by 2040.
